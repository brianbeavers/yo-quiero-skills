¡Yo Quiero! Mode

A Cursor skill that turns one brief into four ambition levels of the same idea.

How the skill works

  1. You give it a PRD or a short brief.
  2. It locks what must stay the same (the job, hard constraints).
  3. It writes four takes of that same idea: Mild, Hot, Spicy, Inferno.
  4. Each take differs by impact, eng effort, complexity, new components/motion, and how bluesky it is.
  5. You pick a heat (or mix them), then deepen that direction.

Invoke with yo-quiero-mode. Pair with yo-quiero-labeling to stamp heats on Figma frames.

Tiny example

Brief: “Help people leave a tip after checkout.” Same problem in every column. Only ambition changes.

Build cost at the bottom of each card is a rough guess of working days for eng and design (focused build time). Not calendar days waiting on reviews, launches, or other teams.

Mild

Add a tip field

A tip amount field on the existing checkout form. Defaults to none.

  • Impact: small
  • New UI: one input
  • Motion: none
  • Risk: low

Eng + design working time About 1 day

Hot

Tip presets

Quick picks (10% / 15% / 20% / custom) under the total, with a clear selected state.

  • Impact: clearer conversion
  • New UI: chip group
  • Motion: light select
  • Risk: low to medium

Eng + design working time About 2 to 4 days

Spicy

Smart tip step

A short post-pay tip screen with suggested amounts based on order size, plus a thank-you moment.

  • Impact: new step in flow
  • New UI: screen + logic
  • Motion: enter/exit
  • Risk: medium

Eng + design working time About 1 to 2 weeks

Inferno

Tip as social layer

Stretch: tip pools, crew shout-outs, optional public notes. Feels like a product of its own.

  • Impact: new product surface
  • New UI: several systems
  • Motion: richer
  • Risk: high / bluesky

Multi-sprint stretch

Point of the board: show how the skill ladders one idea. Not a full product design.
